FAQ

The questions that decide the project.

If yours is not here, ask it directly. A one-line email is enough.

Do I need a 3D model?

No. Send phone photos of the product against a plain background, plus dimensions and any logo files. If the shape is complex, I will quote modelling before starting. If you have a CAD or GLB file, even better.

Can you work from a phone photo?

Yes, for most hard-surface products. A sharp, evenly lit phone photo that shows the edges is enough to model from. For fabric, transparent glass or very glossy surfaces, a short video walkaround helps.

Is this AI or photography?

Neither, and both. The product is built and lit in 3D — that is what keeps angles, materials and labels consistent. AI is used for cleanup, upscaling and variant generation after the render is approved, never to invent the product.

How fast can you deliver?

Single Hero is 4–5 business days from an approved brief and references. Launch Set is 5–7. Campaign is 10–14. Rush work is possible at +35% if the queue allows.

What does a revision include?

Changes to light, framing, background, colour and grade. A new product, a new angle or a new concept is new scope, quoted separately. Revisions are collected in one round so the set stays consistent.

Who owns the images?

You do, for the channels named in the quote, once the final invoice clears. I keep the right to show the work in this portfolio unless we agree otherwise in writing.

Do you do motion?

Yes. Campaign includes a 10-second seamless loop. Longer edits, voice-over and sound design are quoted per project.

What is the free render?

One product, one angle, one 1200px preview with a small watermark, delivered in three business days. It is a real render, not a filter. One per brand, and you keep it for evaluation only.

Can I pause the subscription?

Yes, after the first three months, with 30 days' notice. Work already in the queue is finished and billed. Unused requests do not roll over.
