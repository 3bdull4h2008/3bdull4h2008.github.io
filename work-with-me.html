Straight answers

The questions people ask before signing.

Why not just use AI image generation?

Because the product has to be the same product in every frame. 3D gives me the actual geometry, labels and colour; AI helps with the parts that do not touch product truth.

Do I need to ship you the product?

No. Most projects start and finish from photos. For transparent or highly reflective products, a five-second video turning the object over removes most of the guesswork.

What if I do not like the first frame?

That is what the preview is for. One round of mark-up on the first frame is included before the set renders. If the direction is wrong, we fix it before spending the budget.

Can you match my existing photography?

Usually. Send the previous images and the brand guide. I match lens, light direction, background value and grade, then test against one frame.

Who owns the work?

You license the finals for the channels in the quote once paid. I retain portfolio rights unless we agree otherwise.
