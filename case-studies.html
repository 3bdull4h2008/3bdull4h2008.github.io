Case studies

Four studies, and the decisions behind them.

Four self-directed product studies: a CPU cooler before and after, a watch study, a burger campaign and a perfume bottle. The work is the lighting, materials and composition — not a client claim.

CPU cooler

PC hardware · before / after

Fins, heat pipes and a fan: mostly about controlling specular edges so the stack does not turn to mush.

Before · wireframe / topology
After · final / lit

Notes Rendered from a reference model with custom materials and lighting; the wireframe pass is included because the topology is what decides the highlights.

Unsolicited practice work. No brand has commissioned, supplied or endorsed this piece.

Watch study

Wristwatch · macro and lighting

A second watch exercise, lit to read the dial texture and the bracelet finish at once.

Notes Same case as the dive watch, different set of angles on a white ground and one longer grade pass.

Depicts a Grand Seiko diver. Unsolicited — Grand Seiko has not commissioned or endorsed this render.

Burger scene

Food advertising · trailer styling

A moving piece rather than a still. The same product logic, cut to a trailer rhythm and graded flat and grainy so a rendered burger reads like a photograph somebody took.

Notes Frame-numbered out of the scene, then cut, with the price tags and the camera overlays drawn on afterwards. The conceit is a security camera that keeps zooming into a burger it cannot believe.

A self-directed style study in the manner of a game trailer. It depicts a burger and a cola cup; the brands shown have not commissioned, supplied or endorsed it.

Perfume bottle

Fragrance · dark studio

Glass is a transmission problem before it is a material problem. The interior light has to exist or the bottle reads as plastic.

Notes Lit with an internal source so the liquid glows through the glass rather than being faked in post.
